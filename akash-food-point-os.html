How to Use — Akash Food Point OS

Dashboard

Your at-a-glance summary for today only (resets each morning — history lives in Close Day).

  • Footfall — tap + / − every time someone walks in. Tap it up manually; it also auto-increases when you complete an order for a walk-in who wasn't already counted.
  • Buying / Conversion — Buying = number of completed orders today. Conversion = Buying ÷ Footfall, i.e. what share of people who walked in actually bought something.
  • Sales — sum of Grand Total across today's completed orders.
  • Orders — how many orders completed today. AOV (Average Order Value) = Sales ÷ Orders.
  • Est. Profit — today's (Sales − Food/Packaging Cost) minus today's logged Expenses.
  • Cash / UPI — today's sales split by payment method chosen at checkout.
  • Udhar Out — total still owed by customers across all time (all udhar orders ever, minus all payments ever recorded) — not just today.
  • Expenses / Purchases — today's totals from those two sections.
  • Low Stock — lists any Inventory item whose Stock Left has dropped to or below its Minimum Stock.

New Order

Tap the + on an item to add one, − to remove one. Items with Half/Full sizes show two rows. Long-press a row to type an exact quantity. Set Source, Payment Method, and an optional Discount, then Complete Order — this saves the order, updates today's Sales/Profit, and (if the item has a Recipe) deducts ingredients from Inventory automatically.

Menu Management

Add Categories, then Items inside them. Each item needs a Full price (offline + online) and a Food Cost — that's what profit is calculated from. Packaging Cost only applies to Online orders (Zomato/Swiggy) since dine-in/counter orders don't need a box or bag — the New Order screen automatically drops packaging cost from the profit calc when Source is set to Offline. Turn on "Has Half & Full sizes" to sell both portions separately. The optional Recipe section links an item to Inventory ingredients so stock deducts automatically when it sells, and can auto-fill Food Cost for you. The Offline/Online/Both toggle plus "Download Menu PDF" build a clean printable menu — Offline for what's stuck in your shop, Online to match what's listed on Zomato/Swiggy, or Both to show both prices side by side.

More → Inventory

Tracks raw materials (oil, buns, packaging, etc).

  • Stock Left — the current running balance. You don't edit this number directly.
  • Opening Stock (new item) / Add New Stock (existing item) — the number you type here is added to Stock Left when you save. Stock also drops automatically whenever a recipe-linked menu item is sold.
  • Minimum Stock — not a stock amount, it's an alert threshold. When Stock Left falls to or below this, it shows up under "Low Stock" on the Dashboard.
  • Purchase Cost — cost per unit, used to calculate Recipe Cost on menu items. It updates automatically to your latest Purchase Entry rate.
  • "Recipes use a smaller unit than I buy in" — turn this on when your buying unit and cooking unit differ, e.g. you buy Burger Buns by the dozen but a recipe needs 1 piece. Set Recipe Unit ("piece") and how many of those per 1 purchase unit ("12"). Recipe rows for this item then ask for pieces, not dozens, and selling stock correctly drops fractions of a dozen behind the scenes.

Tap the × on any item to delete it — useful for removing something added by mistake.

More → Purchases

Log every time you buy raw materials. Just type the item name — existing items (like Burger Buns, Noodles Pack, Vegetables) autocomplete as you type; typing a name that doesn't exist yet creates it automatically when you save. Once you've typed a name that matches an existing item, an "Edit" button appears next to it to open that item's full Inventory details. Enter Quantity and Rate (Amount fills in automatically) and save — that adds the quantity to Stock Left and updates Purchase Cost. The card at the top switches between Today / This Week / This Month totals, with a breakdown by item, so recurring buys (like gas or vegetables bought several times a month) visibly add up over time. Made a mistake? Tap the × on any entry in Recent Purchases — it deletes the entry and automatically removes the stock it had added back out of Inventory.

More → Expenses

Log every expense as its own entry — if you buy gas 3 times a month, that's 3 separate entries with their own dates. Nothing needs combining manually. The "This Month" card at the top adds up everything logged this calendar month, broken down by category, so you can see the running total build up. Today's expenses reduce today's Profit on the Dashboard and in Close Day. Tap the × on any entry in Recent Expenses to delete a mistaken entry.

More → Customers

Built automatically from any order where you entered a name or mobile number — no manual setup needed. Shows visits, total spend, average bill, and Udhar balance. Tap "Ledger" on a customer to see their payment history and record a payment against what they owe (this reduces their Udhar balance and the Dashboard's Udhar Out figure).

More → Reports

Opens with Today vs Yesterday and This Month vs Last Month comparisons (with % change, green for up, red for down), a 14-day sales trend chart, then a Monthly Detail section where you pick any month — past or present — to see that month's Sales, Orders, Profit and Expenses, Top Selling Items, a Sales-by-Category breakdown, and every named customer who ordered that month with visits and spend.

More → Seasonal Biz

A completely separate ledger for a different, seasonal product line — not linked to your food menu, recipes, or inventory. Log Purchase and Log Sale work like Purchases in the main app: type any item name (it remembers names you've used before), quantity, unit, and rate — amount fills in automatically. Both now have a Payment Method, so marking something Udhar shows up in the Udhar tab. Summary shows This Month and All Time totals for Sales, Purchases, and Profit, plus a breakdown of what sold this month. Use the × on any entry to delete a mistake.

More → Udhar

One place to see all credit across both businesses. Customers Who Owe You combines anyone marked Udhar on a food order or a Seasonal Biz sale — enter the same mobile number in both places and they merge into one balance instead of two. Each row shows the combined total plus a breakdown ("Food ₹70 · Seasonal ₹60") so you can see both businesses at a glance without opening the ledger. Suppliers You Owe works the same way, combining food and Seasonal purchases marked Udhar. Tap "Ledger" on either to open it. Inside, Pending Entries lists each individual Udhar purchase or sale separately, labeled by which business it's from, with its own date — tap Cleared on one once it's paid, and it's stamped with today's date and drops out of the balance. If a payment doesn't map to one specific entry (a partial or advance payment), use the lump payment field below it instead.

Close Day

A full snapshot of today so far — Footfall, Customers Attended, Orders, Average Order Value, Sales split by Offline vs Online (Zomato/Swiggy), Cash/UPI/Udhar split, Expenses, Purchases, and Profit. Tapping "Close Day" saves this snapshot to the Past Closings history below. You can re-close later in the day to update the saved snapshot if more orders come in. Tap View on any past closing to see its full breakdown, not just the sales total.

Settings (⚙)

Shop Details — change your shop name or currency symbol here; it updates the top bar, printed menu, and PDF menu everywhere at once. Appearance — switch between Dark (default, easier to read under bright counter lighting) and Light. All your data lives only in this browser on this device — nothing is backed up automatically. Export Backup downloads everything as a JSON file; do this regularly. Restore from Backup loads a previously exported file back in, replacing whatever is currently on the device — use this if you switch phones or reinstall. Export to Excel (CSV) downloads Orders, Purchases, or Expenses as a spreadsheet file for sorting/analysis — for viewing only, not for restoring into the app. Reset All Data permanently wipes everything to start clean.
